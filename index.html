About Us

Every wedding
has a story.

Foto Vision is a world class wedding photography and cinematography boutique, providing wedding photography solutions to our patrons since the past four decades.

We at FV believe that every wedding has a story — and in an attempt to capture the essence of that story, we come up with a theme that best signifies the couple.

In the past 45 years we have shot more than 3000+ weddings across the globe, including Germany, Switzerland, Thailand, Indonesia, Dubai, Goa, Udaipur, Jaipur, Varanasi and Mysore. Our team of 75+ professionals — Creative Head, Art Director, Story Teller, Sound & Visual Artists, Candid Photographers and Film Makers — work together to give you the best of wedding documentary.

What we do

Photography & Cinematography

Photography

Photographs are the building blocks of an unfolding story. Our skilled candid photographers create a sincere and intimate space, where people feel so relaxed they won't even know they are being photographed. Using stunning angles and perfectly framed shots, your wedding day memories will live on with you forever.

Cinematography

We understand that wedding films sometimes can be cheesy — which is why we have worked tirelessly to perfect wedding cinematography and deliver narrative films to our customers. Every frame is shot with intent, cut to music, and built around the couple's own story.

Destination Weddings

Destination weddings offer a unique and picturesque backdrop for couples to exchange vows away from home. From sandy beaches to majestic mountains, each destination presents a new canvas for us to document the love and joy of the newlyweds.

Pre-Weddings

Pre-wedding shoots are a delightful prelude to the main event. From candid moments of laughter to tender embraces, we artistically frame these moments — turning them into timeless images that reflect the couple's unique bond.

The Founder · 1978

Hetram Gauri

Founder

Embarking on the journey of Foto Vision in 1978 marked the inception of a timeless tale in the realm of wedding photography. With a dream to encapsulate love in every frame, the seeds of Foto Vision were sown with a passion for freezing fleeting moments and turning them into everlasting memories.

Since its humble beginnings, Foto Vision has grown into a beacon of creativity. Each click of the camera lens is a brushstroke in the masterpiece of matrimony — weaving together emotions, romance, and the promise of forever.

With experience spanning over four decades, Harish Gauri stands as the main pillar behind the brand's enduring success. A keen eye for detail and a passion for capturing the essence of love have made his leadership the driving force behind Foto Vision's timeless visual narratives.

Marketing Head

An engineer by profession turned business strategist, Tarun has played a pivotal role in propelling the brand to new heights. Transitioning from engineering to the family business, his innovative marketing strategies and business acumen have elevated Foto Vision's presence in the industry.
